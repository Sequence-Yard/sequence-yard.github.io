Release notes — what changed in each version

What changed in each version released on Google Play, newest first. You can check your version from the DBR-1 logo → About in the app. The user manual explains every feature in detail.

1.1.0 — 2026-11-28

  • Import your own sample packs (LOAD → Import kit…): folders, ZIP, MPC (.xpm / .pgm / .xpn), SFZ, SoundFont and Decent Sampler, with previews, automatic placement by name and folder, and track names.
  • Up to 4 sounds per track (SOUND → LAYER): switch by velocity, cycle, random or stack them.
  • FILTER tab: new vel control makes harder hits brighter.
  • New TOGGLE play mode: hit again to stop (for BGM and long sounds). PAUSE buttons on the PERF screen, and STOP now silences every ringing sound.
  • ENKAI kit and PERF page for party cue sounds.
  • Max sample length in Settings… lets you load sounds of up to 3 or 5 minutes.

Note: importing a sample pack or program brings its sounds, their layout and velocity layers into DBR-1 tracks. Not every setting of the original software or hardware (envelopes, filters, effects, playback details and so on) carries over, so the result is not a 100% reproduction of the original sound.

1.0.3 — 2026-11-23

  • Tablet: with A+B (16 rows) shown, ALL S / ALL M now switch all 16 tracks.
  • New "Save & Share" in the save screen: save and share in one step, so you can send a project or kit to a friend by email or Drive.

1.0.2 — 2026-11-23

  • Export / import Performance layouts (one page or all, with images and MY SKINs).
  • S / M / L / XL text size for name, part and value.
  • Change the cap colour of VINTAGE / DARK / CHROME parts.
  • New default pages 2–4: mixer, MPC-style and MIDI-controller-style pages with panels and photo-like parts (tablet and phone; your current layout is kept).
  • EDIT: resize a part from anywhere along its bottom band.
  • Phone: the mixer's EQ button is easier to find.

1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

The first public release of DBR-1. Thank you to everyone who joined the closed test.

  • Step sequencer with 16 tracks and 100 patterns, song mode, sampling and the GENERATE sound maker
  • Performance screen: place buttons, faders, knobs and a bar/time display across 8 pages, and style them with your own images
  • MIDI in/out and clock, WAV / MP4 / MIDI export, screen recording
  • Many bug fixes found during testing

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
